# Night Flow

A small, original Android 3D traffic-driving game. Tilt the phone to steer through evening traffic, with a calm procedural soundtrack, adjustable graphics and offline play.

## Install

The **Android APK** workflow builds and tests the application. Download **Night-Flow.apk** from the latest successful run's Artifacts section, then open the APK on your Android phone.

This is a playable first version, signed with a development key. It is not a Google Play release. Real Samsung S24 Ultra performance and the feel of the physical tilt controls still need an on-device check.

## Play

- Hold the phone horizontally, comfortably and steadily, then tap **VOZI**. That position becomes the neutral steering angle.
- Tilt left and right. Use **KOČNICA** to slow down or **GAS** for more speed; otherwise the car maintains cruising speed.
- Choose touch arrows in **POSTAVKE** if you prefer.
- **ZEN** offers gentle traffic and recovery after a collision. **KLASIK** and **IZAZOV** end a run on collision.
- Pause to recalibrate steering or change settings.
- Garage: three original car shapes and six paint colours.

## Features

Native OpenGL ES 3 rendering, procedural car meshes, lit city scenery, sunset/night palettes, road reflections, glow, vehicle shadows, chase/hood cameras, frame-rate limits of 30/60/120 FPS, resolution presets, field of view, steering sensitivity and calibration, audio controls, haptics and locally saved records.

All models and musical sequences are generated by this project. No advertising, login, analytics, network access or downloaded assets are used by the game.

## Build

Install JDK 17, Android SDK platform 35/build-tools 35.0.0, and Gradle 8.11.1, then run:

```sh
gradle :app:assembleDebug
gradle :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:lintDebug
```

APK: `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`.

The CI also installs the app on an Android emulator and exercises the renderer, start/pause/resume, controls and settings. Build evidence is uploaded with each run. An emulator does not establish physical-device FPS or sensor behaviour.

Pinned build compatibility: [Android Gradle plugin 8.9 documentation](https://developer.android.com/build/releases/agp-8-9-0-release-notes).

## Project structure

- `MainActivity`: menus, lifecycle, sensors and frame scheduling.
- `GameState`: independent driving and traffic simulation.
- `WorldRenderer` / `Geometry`: GPU rendering and original geometry.
- `SoundEngine`: an original generative 78 BPM soundtrack and driving sounds.
- `HudView`: touch controls and driving instruments.
